Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version: This preview does not collect email addresses or create accounts. City searches, route coordinates and weather sample locations are sent to providers to produce your plan.

What the preview processes

Your selected cities and departure time are held in page memory and sent to the local Routecast API. City search text goes to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Start and end coordinates go to the OSRM demonstration routing service. Sample coordinates go to Open-Meteo for hourly forecasts; Routecast selects the relevant hours locally. OpenStreetMap receives requests for visible map tiles. Providers also receive ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and user agent.

Storage

Routecast has no database, analytics, account system, email platform, payment integration, cookies or saved-route storage. The local development server keeps a bounded, in-memory cache of provider responses (10 minutes for weather and cities; 1 hour for routes). Expired entries can remain in memory until replaced or the server stops, but are not served as fresh. Closing the page clears its trip state; stopping the server clears the server cache. Browser HTTP caches may retain map tiles according to provider cache headers.

Third-party services

Providers have their own retention policies and may keep request logs. Read Open-Meteo terms and privacy, FOSSGIS/OSRM privacy information and the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. This preview uses system fonts and does not request Google Fonts.

Your choices

Use city names rather than private addresses. No precise-device-location permission is requested. Do not enter sensitive information. You can stop a lookup or close the page; a request already sent to a provider may still finish. There is no waitlist or email unsubscribe process because signup collection is disabled.

Future versions

This policy describes this local development version. Public hosting, persistent storage, paid providers or other features require a fresh privacy review before release. The project source and issue tracker are at Karieo/Routecast.
